Overview

Ship Management in Sri Lanka

ARCSHIP provides full and technical ship management for owners and operators with tonnage calling at Sri Lankan ports, combining planned maintenance, crewing, compliance and commercial support under one certified QHSE system.

Management is delivered to ISM, ISPS and MARPOL requirements and governed by ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 systems, with transparent reporting on maintenance, budget and inspection status. In-house engineering means a managed vessel’s technical needs are met by one accountable team rather than a chain of sub-contractors.

  • ISO 9001, 14001 & 45001 certified management
  • ISM, ISPS & MARPOL compliance
  • In-house engineering & emergency response
  • Transparent maintenance & budget reporting

Scope

The seven functions of full management

Technical Management

Planned maintenance, defect rectification, dry-dock planning, spares and stores, and class and statutory survey management.

Crew Management

Recruitment, certification, planning and welfare of competent seafarers manned to flag and STCW requirements.

Financial & Commercial

Budgeting, operating-cost control, accounting and reporting with commercial support that keeps the vessel earning.

Operations

Voyage and port-call coordination, bunkering and the logistics that keep the vessel moving between load and discharge.

Safety & Compliance

ISM and ISPS implementation, incident management, audits and continuous inspection readiness.

Risk Management

Structured identification, assessment and mitigation of operational, technical and commercial risk.

Environmental Management

MARPOL compliance, emissions and waste management and the record-keeping behind environmental performance.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does ARCSHIP offer full or technical management only?

Both. ARCSHIP provides full management covering technical, crew, commercial and operational functions, or technical management alone, depending on the owner’s requirement.

Which standards govern your management systems?

Management is delivered under ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 certified systems and to ISM, ISPS and MARPOL requirements.

Can management include in-house engineering at Sri Lankan ports?

Yes. Managed vessels draw on ARCSHIP’s in-house engine overhaul, mechanical, LSA/FFA and calibration capability for fast attendance at Sri Lankan ports.
