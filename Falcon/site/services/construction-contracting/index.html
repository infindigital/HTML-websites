FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What construction activities does Falcon Rotating undertake?

We undertake civil construction, piping works, industrial maintenance, electrical maintenance, industrial equipment installation, steel fabrication and sheet metal works, carpentry and finish works, and the fabrication and erection of stairs, handrails, beams and columns.

Do you handle both commercial and industrial projects?

Yes. Falcon Rotating was formed to provide general contracting services and subcontract work across commercial and industrial industries.

How do you manage site safety?

We maintain an ongoing safety program and conduct regular meetings to address job-site and personal safety, keeping field personnel informed on schedules and active project matters.
