Nileshwar Sunset Cruise

The Nileshwar Sunset Cruise is our shortest, most affordable voyage, two unhurried hours timed to the best light of the day. Between 4:30pm and 6:30pm the sky turns gold and rose over the water, with evening tea and snacks served on board.

It is a lovely way to end a day in Kasaragod or slow down for an evening. With the houseboat reserved for your group, the sundeck is the best seat for the Nileshwar sunset.
