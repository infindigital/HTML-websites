Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how INFIN Digital collects, uses, shares and protects information when you visit our website or get in touch with us about our web design, branding and digital marketing services.

Last Updated: September 2026

01 Introduction

INFIN Digital (“INFIN Digital”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a web design and digital marketing agency based in Mangaluru, Karnataka, India. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling information responsibly.

This policy applies to information we collect through our website and through direct communications with you. By using our website or contacting us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

02 Information We Collect

We collect information in two broad ways: information you choose to provide to us directly, and information that may be collected automatically when you use our website, depending on the tools and technologies enabled at the time.

03 Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information when you get in touch with us — for example through a contact form, a project inquiry or quote request, email, WhatsApp, a phone call, a newsletter or subscription form (where offered), or other direct communication.

The information you provide may include:

  • Name
  • Company or business name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Project requirements and goals
  • Budget or business information you choose to share
  • Website, brand or project details
  • Any other information you decide to include in your message

Please share only the information that is reasonably necessary for your enquiry. We do not ask you to provide sensitive personal information through our website.

04 Information Collected Automatically

Depending on the tools and technologies enabled on the website, some technical information may be collected automatically when you browse, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and referring pages
  • Approximate location derived from IP address, where applicable
  • Interaction information
  • Date and time of visits

This information is generally used in aggregate to understand website usage and to help us maintain and improve the site.

05 How We Use Information

Where we hold information, we may use it to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide requested information
  • Prepare proposals, quotations and project estimates
  • Provide and manage the services agreed with you
  • Communicate with you about your project or account
  • Operate, maintain, secure and improve our website
  • Understand how our website and marketing perform
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

06 Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies — small files or identifiers stored on your device. In plain terms, these may be used to help the website function, remember preferences, measure performance, and understand how visitors interact with the site.

You can usually control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect how parts of the website work.

07 Website Analytics

As a digital marketing agency, we may use analytics and measurement tools to understand how visitors use our website, improve website performance and evaluate our marketing activity. Where such tools are used, they may rely on the technical information described above.

08 Advertising and Marketing Technologies

INFIN Digital provides advertising and marketing services to its clients. That is separate from the technologies running on this website. We only use advertising or marketing measurement technologies on our own website where they have actually been implemented, and any such use is intended to help us understand and improve our marketing.

09 How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information where reasonably necessary with:

  • Service and technology providers who support our operations
  • Hosting and analytics providers
  • Professional advisers
  • Authorities or third parties where required by law or to protect our rights
  • A successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition or business reorganisation

10 Third-Party Services

Our website and communications may involve third-party services such as hosting providers, analytics providers, email providers, communication platforms and, where applicable, other business tools. These providers process information on our behalf or under their own terms, and their use of information is governed by their respective policies.

11 Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to respond to enquiries, deliver services, maintain business records and meet legal or regulatory requirements. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it.

12 Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13 Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where applicable
  • Withdraw consent to certain communications
  • Ask questions about how your information is handled

Rights may vary depending on applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

14 Marketing Communications

Where you have provided your details, we may occasionally contact you about our services or relevant updates. You can ask us to stop sending marketing communications at any time by contacting us using the details below.

15 External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or platforms. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to review their policies.

16 Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for a general business audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly seek or collect personal information from children through our website.

17 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies or legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the “Last Updated” date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
