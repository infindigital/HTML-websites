Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how INFIN Digital collects, uses, shares and protects information when you visit our website or get in touch with us about our web design, branding and digital marketing services.

Last Updated: September 2026

01 Introduction

INFIN Digital (“we”, “us” or “our”) is a web design and digital marketing agency based in Mangaluru, Karnataka, India. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information collected through our website and when you contact us. By using our website or contacting us, you accept this policy.

02 Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to give us — for example when you use a contact or enquiry form, or reach out by email, WhatsApp or phone. This may include your name, company, email, phone number and project details you decide to share. We do not ask for sensitive personal information.

Depending on the tools enabled on the site, some technical information may also be collected automatically, such as IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, and date and time of visits. This is generally used in aggregate to maintain and improve the website.

03 How We Use Information

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare proposals, provide and manage agreed services, communicate with you, operate and improve our website, understand how our site and marketing perform, and meet legal obligations.

04 Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to help it function, remember preferences and measure performance. As a marketing agency, we may also use analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, though some features may then work differently.

05 How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it where reasonably necessary with service and technology providers (such as hosting and analytics), professional advisers, authorities where required by law, or a successor entity in connection with a business reorganisation.

06 Data Retention and Security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above and to meet legal requirements, after which we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it. We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

07 Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to access, correct or delete your information, withdraw consent to marketing, or ask how your information is handled. Rights may vary depending on applicable law, and we may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

08 Children and External Links

Our website is intended for a general business audience and is not directed at children; we do not knowingly collect information from children. Our site may link to third-party websites whose content and privacy practices are not our responsibility.

09 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will revise the “Last Updated” date above when we do. Please review this page periodically.
