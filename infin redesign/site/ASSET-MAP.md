# INFIN asset inventory & mapping

Every asset from the supplied ZIP was inspected visually before assignment. Large
PNG/WebP source files were re-encoded to optimised WebP (≤1600px, q82) for
performance; small logos/badges/plates kept as supplied. Renamed to semantic names.
