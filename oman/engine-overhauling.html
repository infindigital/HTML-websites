Overview

Marine Engine Overhauling

ARCSHIP overhauls main and auxiliary engines and their associated machinery to original-equipment-maker and classification-society standards, planned around the survey calendar or mobilised as an emergency response.

Work covers dismantling, inspection, measurement, renewal of running gear, reassembly and commissioning, supported by condition reports and test records that protect reliability and asset value.

  • Main & auxiliary engine overhaul
  • Maker- & class-recognised workmanship
  • Planned or emergency attendance
  • Condition reports & measurement records

Scope

Capability

01

Assessment

Review of running hours, performance data and maker recommendations to define the overhaul scope.

02

Dismantling

Controlled dismantling of units with parts tagged, protected and recorded.

03

Inspection & Measurement

Dimensional inspection of components against maker tolerances to identify wear and defects.

04

Renewal

Replacement of bearings, rings, liners and running gear with genuine or approved spares.

05

Reassembly

Reassembly to maker torque and clearance specifications, fully documented.

06

Commissioning

Function testing, trials and performance verification before return to service.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you attend for emergency engine breakdowns?

Yes. ARCSHIP mobilises teams for emergency overhaul and breakdown attendance alongside or at anchorage.

Is overhaul work carried out to maker standards?

Overhauls are completed to original-equipment-maker tolerances and classification-society requirements, with measurement and test records.

Can overhaul be planned around a dry-docking?

Yes. Scope is routinely planned around the survey and docking calendar to minimise off-hire.
