This policy explains how ARCSHIP MARINE SERVICES SPC (“ARCSHIP”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information collected through this website.

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry we may collect the details you provide, such as your name, company, email, phone number and the vessel and scope information in your message. We may also collect standard technical data (for example, browser type and pages viewed) to operate and improve the site.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, prepare proposals, deliver services and maintain our business records. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

Information may be shared with ARCSHIP group entities and trusted service providers acting on our behalf, and where required by law. Any such sharing is limited to what is necessary for the purposes above.

Cookies

This site aims to keep cookie use minimal. Where cookies or similar technologies are used, they support core functionality and basic analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described here or as required by applicable law and record-keeping obligations.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details on our contact page.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact ARCSHIP by email at info@arcship.ae or via the contact page.

Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Questions about this policy? Contact ARCSHIP
