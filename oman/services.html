Our Services

Complete Maritime Services for All Your Needs

ARCSHIP’s maritime services come in a wide range of options for different needs. This comprehensive list includes ship management, life-saving appliance servicing, fire-fighting appliance servicing, engine overhauling, marine mechanical solutions, calibration services, and marine safety products.

As an overview, ship management offers services based on technical, operational, financial, commercial, risk, environmental, safety, and compliance aspects. Meanwhile, life-saving appliances include lifebuoys, lifejackets, protective suits, lifeboats, rescue boats, life rafts, evacuation systems, smoke signals, and alarm systems. ARCSHIP also offers fire-fighting appliances, but they are highlighted in a separate category. The services include fire detection, alarm systems, breathing apparatus, sprinkler systems, protective suits, and various types of extinguishers for different situations.

Additionally, engine overhaul services offer diagnostic analysis, planning, scheduling, disassembly and inspection, component reconditioning, parts replacement, assembly, testing, documentation, and reporting. Marine mechanical solutions also touch on technical aspects, specifically pump overhauling, cooler servicing, and pressure testing.

Calibration services are on the list as well. They cover oil discharge monitoring equipment, oily water separators, gas detectors, fixed gas detection systems, and cargo tank pressure monitoring among other things. Lastly, marine safety products are crucial for the survival of the entire crew. They include emergency food and drinking water.
