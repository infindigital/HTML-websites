01Getting documents to us

How Olathe clients send documents.

Olathe clients can drop documents off at our office, book a mobile notary in Johnson County, or mail documents in with tracking.

Because the Johnson County courthouse is in Olathe, court document apostilles are among our most common Olathe requests, along with MidAmerica Nazarene University transcripts.

02Certified copies

Where to get certified copies in Johnson County.

An apostille can only be placed on the right version of a document. These are the local offices that issue it.

  1. 01

    Court documents

    The Johnson County District Court is in Olathe. Order certified copies of divorce decrees and court orders from its clerk.

  2. 02

    Birth certificates

    The KDHE Office of Vital Statistics in Topeka issues certified Kansas birth certificates.

  3. 03

    Marriage certificates

    KDHE Vital Statistics, or the Johnson County District Court if the license was issued there.

05FAQ

Yes. Order a certified copy from the Johnson County District Court clerk in Olathe and we submit it to the Kansas Secretary of State.

Yes. Olathe is in our Kansas City metro area for mobile notary.
