What is in the scene

Torus knot core

A physically shaded torus knot with metalness and roughness, lit by three coloured lights that orbit it continuously.

Instanced field

Hundreds of small cubes drawn in a single instanced mesh, each one drifting and spinning on its own offset.

Scroll camera

The camera position is driven by scroll progress and eased every frame, so the page reads as one continuous flight.
