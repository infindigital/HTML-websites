Overview

Life-Saving Appliance (LSA) Servicing in Saudi Arabia

ARCSHIP services the full range of life-saving appliances to SOLAS and the LSA Code — from lifeboats, liferafts and davits to lifejackets, immersion suits and pyrotechnics — with test certificates issued on completion.

Attendances are scheduled into a vessel’s Saudi port call at Jeddah, Yanbu, Dammam, Jubail or Ras Tanura, keeping survival equipment in date and the vessel inspection-ready for port-state control.

  • SOLAS & LSA Code compliant servicing
  • Annual inspection & five-yearly load testing
  • Liferaft & lifejacket re-certification
  • Test certificates & service reports issued

Scope

Life-saving equipment we service

Lifeboats & Rescue Boats

Inspection, function testing and servicing of totally enclosed lifeboats, free-fall boats and rescue boats.

Liferafts & Evacuation Systems

Servicing and re-packing of inflatable liferafts and marine evacuation systems at approved intervals.

Lifebuoys & Lifejackets

Inspection, testing and re-certification of lifebuoys, lifejackets and their lights and equipment.

Immersion & Anti-Exposure Suits

Air-pressure and seam testing, inspection and re-certification of immersion suits and thermal protective aids.

Emergency Alarm & Communication

Testing of general and emergency alarm systems and associated survival-craft communication equipment.

Pyrotechnics & Line-Throwing

Supply and replacement of parachute and hand flares, buoyant smoke signals and line-throwing apparatus.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How often must life-saving appliances be serviced?

Crew checks are weekly and monthly; professional inspection and function testing is annual; davits, winches and survival craft are load tested five-yearly. Exact intervals depend on equipment type and flag rules.

Do you carry out davit load testing?

Yes. ARCSHIP performs load and overload testing of davits, winches and launching appliances, with certificates issued on completion.

Which Saudi ports do you attend for LSA work?

Jeddah, King Abdullah Port and Yanbu on the Red Sea, and Dammam, Jubail and Ras Tanura on the Arabian Gulf.
