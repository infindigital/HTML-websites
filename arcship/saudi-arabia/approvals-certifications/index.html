Codes & conventions

The standards our work is delivered to

Servicing, management and compliance carried out to the international conventions and codes that govern vessels trading in the Kingdom.

01

SOLAS

International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea, the framework behind our safety-equipment servicing.

02

MARPOL

Prevention of pollution from ships, governing environmental compliance and record-keeping.

03

ISM Code

Safety management of ships and pollution prevention, implemented across managed vessels.

04

ISPS Code

Ship and port-facility security requirements, built into management and audits.

05

LSA Code

Life-Saving Appliance Code, the standard for inspection and servicing of lifeboats, davits and liferafts.

06

FSS Code

Fire Safety Systems Code, governing fixed and portable fire-fighting appliance servicing.

07

MLC 2006

Maritime Labour Convention, underpinning crew welfare in ship management.

08

STCW

Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for competent, certified seafarers.
