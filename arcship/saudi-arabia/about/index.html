About ARCSHIP

A single technical partner for vessels trading in Saudi Arabia

ARC SHIP MANAGEMENT & CHARTERING supports shipowners, operators and charterers with in-house marine engineering and safety-equipment servicing, delivered to Saudi ports on both the Red Sea and Arabian Gulf coasts.

Who we are

In-house marine engineering, delivered across the Kingdom

From full technical and crew management to LSA and FFA servicing, engine overhaul, calibration and SOLAS-approved safety provisions, ARCSHIP delivers a complete marine engineering capability under one roof.

Work is carried out under a single QHSE system and to the standards of leading classification societies and flag states. The Kingdom's operations are supported by ARC Ship Equipment Maintenance Co., an ISO 9001:2015-certified entity in the Eastern Province, so a managed vessel's technical needs are met by one accountable team rather than a chain of sub-contractors.

  • ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 certified management systems
  • SOLAS, MARPOL, LSA and FSS Code compliant servicing
  • Class- and maker-recognised marine engineering
  • 24/7 emergency attendance at Saudi ports and anchorages

Our mission

To deliver compliant, in-house marine engineering and safety-equipment servicing that keeps owners' vessels safe, certified and trading across Saudi Arabia's ports and anchorages.

Our vision

To be the single accountable technical partner for vessels operating on the Kingdom's Red Sea and Arabian Gulf coasts, recognised for safety, compliance and responsiveness.

What we stand for

The values behind every attendance

01

Safety first

Every attendance is planned around the safety of crew, vessel and our own teams, under a documented QHSE system.

02

Compliance by default

Work is delivered to SOLAS, MARPOL, ISM, ISPS and the LSA and FSS Codes, and to classification-society and flag-state requirements.

03

One accountable team

In-house engineering means a vessel's technical and safety needs are met by one responsible partner, not a chain of sub-contractors.

04

Fast response

Teams mobilise to Saudi ports and anchorages on both coasts, with round-the-clock attendance for urgent defects.

05

Class recognition

Marine engineering and safety servicing carried out to the standards of leading classification societies and equipment makers.

06

Environmental care

MARPOL compliance, waste and emissions management and the record-keeping behind responsible operations.
