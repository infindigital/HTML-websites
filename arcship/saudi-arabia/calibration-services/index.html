Overview

Marine Calibration Services

ARCSHIP calibrates the shipboard instruments that safety and compliance depend on, from portable gas detectors to pressure, temperature and level instrumentation, with traceable calibration certificates.

Calibration keeps monitoring, alarm and navigation systems accurate and inspection-ready, and is scheduled into the vessel’s Saudi port call.

  • Traceable calibration certificates
  • Class- & flag-recognised procedures
  • Portable gas detector calibration
  • Instrument & safety-system calibration

Scope

Instruments & systems we calibrate

01

Portable Gas Detectors

Calibration of personal and portable multi-gas and oxygen detectors.

02

Fixed Gas Detection

Calibration of fixed gas and oxygen analyser systems and sampling lines.

03

Pressure Instruments

Pressure gauges, transmitters and switches across machinery and cargo systems.

04

Temperature Sensors

Temperature sensors, transmitters and thermostatic controls.

Level & Tank Gauging

Level gauges and tank-gauging and monitoring systems.

Calibration of shipboard test and measuring equipment.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide traceable calibration certificates?

Yes. Calibration is carried out to recognised procedures with traceable certificates issued for each instrument.

Can you calibrate portable gas detectors on board?

Yes. Personal and portable multi-gas and oxygen detectors are calibrated during the port attendance.

Is calibration recognised by class and flag?

Calibration follows classification-society and flag-state requirements; specific scope is confirmed on request.
