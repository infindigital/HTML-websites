About ARCSHIP in Saudi Arabia

A single technical partner for vessels trading in the Kingdom

ARC SHIP MANAGEMENT & CHARTERING supports shipowners, operators and charterers with in-house marine engineering and safety-equipment servicing, delivered to Saudi ports on both the Red Sea and Arabian Gulf coasts.

From full technical and crew management to LSA and FFA servicing, engine overhaul, calibration and SOLAS-approved safety provisions, work is carried out under one QHSE system and to the standards of leading classification societies and flag states. The Kingdom’s operations are supported by ARC Ship Equipment Maintenance Co., an ISO 9001:2015-certified entity in the Eastern Province.

  • ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 certified management systems
  • SOLAS, MARPOL, LSA and FSS Code compliant servicing
  • Class- and maker-recognised marine engineering
  • 24/7 emergency attendance at Saudi ports and anchorages

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which Saudi ports does ARCSHIP cover?

ARCSHIP supports vessels calling at the Kingdom’s principal commercial and industrial ports, Jeddah and King Abdullah Port on the Red Sea, and Dammam, Jubail and Ras Tanura on the Arabian Gulf, as well as offshore fields and anchorages on both coasts.

Are your life-saving and fire-fighting services SOLAS compliant?

Yes. Life-saving appliance and fire-fighting appliance servicing is carried out to SOLAS, the LSA and FSS Codes and the relevant flag-state and classification-society requirements, with test certificates issued on completion.

Can ARCSHIP attend a vessel during a short port call?

Teams are mobilised to meet the vessel’s schedule. Where scope is advised in advance, spares and test equipment are pre-positioned so inspection, repair and re-certification are completed within the port stay.

Is ARCSHIP approved by classification societies?

ARCSHIP works to the standards of leading classification societies including Lloyd’s Register, Bureau Veritas, ABS, Korean Register, RINA, ClassNK, the Indian Register of Shipping, CCS and Tasneef. Approval scope by society is confirmed on request.

What vessel types do you service?

Oil and chemical tankers, offshore support vessels, container ships, bulk carriers and general cargo vessels, among others.

How do I request a quotation?

Send the vessel particulars, port and scope of work through the contact form or WhatsApp. A technical response with scope and indicative timeline follows.

Planning a port call in Saudi Arabia?

Send your vessel particulars, port and scope, we’ll respond with a technical proposal and indicative timeline.
