## 1. Per template — REQUIRED (minimum to go live)

### a) Hero film (the cinematic video)
- **Format:** MP4 — H.264 video + AAC audio
- **Frame / ratio:** 1920×1080, **16:9 landscape**
- **Length:** 20–30 seconds (your brief targets ~00:26)
- **FPS:** 24 or 30
- **Quality:** send the best master you have; I compress for web (target < ~12 MB, still crisp)
- **Content:** follow your own Scene 01–07 brief for that template; bake in the demo couple names above
- **Filename:** `noor.mp4` · `aaranya.mp4` · `celeste.mp4`

### b) Poster still (shown before the video loads)
- **Format:** WEBP or JPG
- **Frame / ratio:** 1920×1080 (16:9) — a first frame or a hero moment from the film
- **Filename:** `noor-poster.webp` (or `.jpg`) · `aaranya-poster.webp` · `celeste-poster.webp`

## 2. Per template — RECOMMENDED

### c) Vertical film (mobile fullscreen)
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 + AAC), **1080×1920, 9:16**, same film reframed
- **Filename:** `noor-vertical.mp4` · `aaranya-vertical.mp4` · `celeste-vertical.mp4`
- If skipped: mobile plays the 16:9 film inside a cinematic letterboxed frame — still fine.

### d) Music (ONLY if your film has no music baked in)
- **Format:** MP3, 128–192 kbps, stereo, ≥30s
- **Style:** instrumental, **no vocals**, royalty-free / owned / licensed
  - Noor: soft oud, atmospheric strings, subtle percussion
  - Aaranya: sitar, soft tabla, orchestral strings
  - Celeste: soft piano, orchestral strings, subtle wordless choir
- **Filename:** `noor.mp3` · `aaranya.mp3` · `celeste.mp3`
- If your film already includes its audio, skip this.
