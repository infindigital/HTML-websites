# Generation prompt pack - Noor / Aaranya / Celeste

Copy-paste prompts for producing every asset with AI tools. Built from your
scene briefs. Three distinct worlds, one premium brand.

## Recommended tools
- Video: Kling 2.x, Runway Gen-3/4, Luma Dream Machine, Pika, or Higgsfield (Plus plan). Make 5s clips, stitch to ~26s in CapCut / Premiere / DaVinci Resolve.
- Poster image: Midjourney v6/v7, Ideogram (best if you want text baked in), Leonardo, DALL-E, or Higgsfield.
- Music: Suno v4, Udio, or ElevenLabs Music. Instrumental only.

## Global rules
- Generate visuals WITHOUT text. AI misspells names. Add the names/dates in your editor, OR send me the clean footage and I overlay the text crisply in code.
- Demo names only (never any other names): Noor = Rayyan & Inaya, Aaranya = Aarav & Ananya, Celeste = Nathan & Grace.
- Aspect: 16:9 for desktop. Also make a 9:16 version of the hero scenes for mobile if the tool allows.
- Duration per clip: 5s, 24fps, 1080p or higher.
- Global NEGATIVE (append to every video + image prompt):
  `text, words, letters, captions, subtitles, watermark, signature, logo, deformed, distorted, blurry, low quality, extra limbs, cartoon, oversaturated, cluttered, modern clothing, brand names`
- Poster note: text-free posters look best on the cards (the title shows below the image). Want the title on the artwork instead? Use Ideogram or add it in an editor.

Stitch order for each film: Scene 1 > 2 > 3 > (details/story backdrop) > architecture/venue > final. Aim ~26s total.
