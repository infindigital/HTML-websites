Welcome to MASKO Contracting Company

Reliable support for industrial projects

MASKO Contracting Company operates across Saudi Arabia, delivering the contracting services, equipment, and materials that keep industrial and commercial projects running. We’ve built our business on a straightforward principle: when contractors and project teams need something, we make sure they get it without the runaround.

Since our establishment, we’ve worked with construction firms, industrial operators, maintenance teams, and project managers who need suppliers they can count on. We’re not here to complicate things with layers of bureaucracy or make promises we can’t keep. We’re here to provide what projects need, when they need it, with the quality and reliability that separates professional operations from everyone else.

MASKO has built its reputation by being available when it counts, maintaining equipment that actually works, and supplying workers who know what they’re doing. We stock the materials projects consume, maintain a comprehensive equipment fleet, and deploy manpower that shows up ready to work.
