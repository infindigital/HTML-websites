Equipment Spare Parts

MASKO Contracting Company supplies genuine and compatible spare parts for industrial equipment and heavy machinery operating across Saudi Arabia. From hydraulic seals and engine components to gearboxes, filters, and control modules, our spare parts inventory supports the maintenance work that keeps equipment running and reduces costly downtime.

We work with equipment manufacturers and OEM distributors to ensure the parts we supply are authentic and perform as expected. When equipment breaks down, waiting weeks for the right spare part can shut down entire operations. Our inventory and delivery capability are built around minimizing that waiting time, so your maintenance teams can get machinery back in service quickly instead of dealing with prolonged delays and mounting costs.
