Manpower Supply

MASKO Contracting Company supplies skilled and dependable manpower to meet the workforce requirements of industrial and construction projects across Saudi Arabia. Our workforce includes certified technicians, welders, riggers, safety officers, equipment operators, and experienced laborers who have been properly screened and trained. We focus on deploying personnel who understand not just the technical requirements but also the importance of showing up on time, working safely, and functioning as part of a team on active job sites.

Whether you need workers for short-term maintenance shutdowns, ongoing operations, or long-term deployment on major projects, our manpower solutions adapt to your specific needs. We can scale our workforce up or down based on project phases, helping you avoid the costs and complications of direct hiring while maintaining the productivity and safety standards your project demands. Our teams show up ready to work, understand the job requirements, and stay engaged until the work is complete.
