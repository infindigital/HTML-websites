Equipment Rental

MASKO Contracting Company maintains a comprehensive fleet of heavy-duty equipment available for rent throughout Saudi Arabia. We supply cranes, boom lifts, forklifts, generators, welding machines, air compressors, and earth-moving equipment that's been properly maintained and inspected. What separates our rental service is simple: the equipment actually works when it arrives on your site. We service our fleet consistently because we know breakdowns don't just delay one task; they can derail your entire operation. You're not getting machinery that creates more problems than it solves.

Whether you need operated rentals with skilled operators or non-operated equipment for your own teams, we adapt to your project needs. Short-term deployment or extended rental periods, we handle both. When complications arise, we're responsive with maintenance support instead of making you chase us down for answers. You get working equipment, straightforward communication, and suppliers who stay available throughout your rental period. That's how we've built our equipment rental reputation across the Kingdom.
